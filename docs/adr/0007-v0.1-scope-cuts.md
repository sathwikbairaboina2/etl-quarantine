# ADR 0007: v0.1 scope: replay in, analytics and the LLM out

Status: accepted, 2026-10-04

## Context

The design doc's v0.1 milestone leaves replay to v0.2, but the portfolio pitch is "quarantine, fix and replay with one command", and the 30-second demo needs that loop. The doc's v0.1 also assumes LocalStack and an Athena path that are not available (ADR 0002).

## Decision

- **In v0.1:** human fixes (`etl quarantine fix`, `etl quarantine discard`), `etl replay` (all quarantined rows, or `--only-fixed`), lineage edges and row states, the `SUPERSEDED` status, and `etl promote`.
- **Out of v0.1:**
  - Glue tables, the Athena workgroup and `etl athena`. `etl curated count` reads Parquet locally instead.
  - The LLM fix suggester and `fixes/proposed/`.
  - JSONL input, and `etl schema diff` with its CI gate.
  - SNS alerts and the EventBridge archive.
- **No cost-per-million-rows number** is published, because nothing was deployed and measured. The headline is conservation under faults plus local throughput, both measured by `npm run bench`.

## Consequences

- The demo shows the full fix-and-replay loop, and invariants I1-I8 and I11 have tests.
- What we gave up: I9 (schema-diff gate) and I10 (Athena scan cutoff) are not implemented, and the design's cost metric has no number yet. The README lists both under "Known limits".
